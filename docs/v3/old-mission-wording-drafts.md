# Groundwork v3: replacing the wording that talks about missions and step numbers

**Status: drafts for David's review, 14 Nov 2026 (D60). Nothing in the app has changed. The drafts are also held in `old-mission-wording.changes.json`, so that once you approve them they can be applied exactly as written.**

The step and week screens now show the part of the map they live in, but the content still says things like "Look back at the strengths you mapped in Mission 1". This page proposes a replacement for every such line a student can still see.

## How I drafted them

- **The smallest change that works.** Only the words that mention a mission, a step number or "nine weeks" change. The rest of each sentence is yours, untouched.
- **A step is named by its own title.** "In Mission 1" becomes "in The Mask Check" or "in Strengths Mapping", so the student knows exactly where to look.
- **Where a whole mission was meant,** I've used the name it now carries on the map: Identity, Purpose or Connection, or the level (Know myself).
- **Nothing implies an order.** "Mission 1 looked inward. This one looks outward" becomes "A lot of Groundwork looks inward. This one looks outward."

## Things to check

1. **Steps that point back at another step** (the second group above) read as if the earlier step has been done. That was true in v2, where the order was fixed. On the map a student can arrive without it. The screens already cope: the earlier answer is shown when it exists and the step works without it. But a line like "In The Mask Check you noticed how you change between rooms" will be wrong for a student who hasn't done it. I've kept your sentences; say if you'd prefer "If you've done The Mask Check, …".
2. **"A lot of Groundwork looks inward" / "is about you"** replaces four different openings. It's plain. You may have a better line.
3. **The Through-Line** now refers to Identity, Purpose and Connection, the names those three groups carry on the map. Its three cards are still built from v2's three missions, which is a separate piece of work (see the level map, section 6).
4. **"It's a milestone letter in Value myself"** (#55) and **"No single step builds this one"** (#64) are my wording, not a trim of yours.
5. **One more I noticed and didn't list:** the note under #64 says "weeks 5 and 7 are where it gets built". The weeks keep their numbers, so I've left it.
