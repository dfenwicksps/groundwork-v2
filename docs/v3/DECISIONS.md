# Groundwork v3: decision log

Every decision that shapes v3 gets a numbered entry here: the date, the decision, the reason, and what it means for the build. Entries are never rewritten. A decision that changes gets a new entry that names the one it replaces.

- **D1–D11** are David's, copied from section 1 of [SPEC.md](SPEC.md). Where the spec doesn't record a reason, this log says so and doesn't supply one.
- **D12 onwards** were made during the build. "Agent" in the *By* column means the coding agent made the call and David hasn't yet confirmed it; these are open to reversal at review.
